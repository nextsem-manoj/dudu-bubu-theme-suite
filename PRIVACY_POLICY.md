# Privacy Policy for Bubu & Dudu Wallpaper & Theme App

**Last updated:** October 2026

Thank you for choosing the **Bubu & Dudu Wallpaper & Theme App** ("App", "we", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Our app is designed purely to provide personal customization wallpapers and themes for your device:
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, location, or device identifiers).
- **No Account Required:** You can use all features of the App without creating an account or logging in.
- **Offline Functionality:** The App operates completely on your local device and does not send any data to external servers.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The App requests the following minimal permissions necessary only for core functionality:
- `SET_WALLPAPER` & `SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS`: Required solely to apply the selected wallpaper to your device's Home Screen and Lock Screen when you click the "Apply" button.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking libraries, or advertising networks that collect user data.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App does not address anyone under the age of 13 for data collection because we do not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, including children.

---

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at your support email address.
